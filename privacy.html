BudgetAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Chrome extension "BudgetAI – AI Subscription Tracker"

Summary

BudgetAI is a monitoring tool for your own AI subscriptions. All of your data stays in your browser. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. We never sell or share data.

What the extension stores, and where

  • The subscriptions you add or confirm: tool name, price, billing cycle, renewal date, credits.
  • Plan details shown on an AI tool's own billing page when you open it (plan name, price, renewal date, credits).
  • The date you last opened each supported AI tool's website — one date per tool, used only to tell you about subscriptions you pay for but don't use.
  • Your settings and the history of reminders already shown.

Everything is saved with Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. Nothing is transmitted to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not access bank accounts, card numbers or payment credentials.
  • It does not read pages outside the listed AI tools' websites, and does not read form fields.
  • It never buys, cancels or pays anything on your behalf. Buttons only open the vendor's own page.

Gmail (optional, only where enabled)

If you choose to connect Gmail, BudgetAI requests read-only access and searches only for billing receipts from AI tools and payment processors. Messages are processed locally in your browser to find subscriptions and renewal dates. Email content is never stored on or sent to any server, and the extension cannot send, change or delete email. You can disconnect at any time in the extension's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. BudgetAI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
